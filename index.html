    L-09 · FAQ

    Questions

    What is a Seed Project?

    Your first project with us, at a founding price of with scope. One per company, paid upfront.

    Is the work white-label?

    Yes. We deliver under your logo, or co-branded if you prefer. We never contact your clients unless you ask us to.

    Who owns the designs?

    You do. Final files are yours once the project is paid in full.

    What counts as a revision?

    One revision round is one consolidated list of changes, sent together. Packages include . Extra rounds are each. A new layout is a redesign, .

    How fast is turnaround?

    From the day we have complete site info: Sprout , Grove , Canopy . Rush delivery is .

    What do you need from me?

    Site photos, rough measurements or a survey, your client's wish list, and a budget range. More detail means fewer revisions.

    Can you work from photos only?

    Yes. We work from your photos and approximate dimensions, and the plan is drawn to those. You verify measurements on site before pricing and building.

    What software do you use?

    Edmond designs in Realtime Landscaping Architect and SketchUp, and renders in Lumion and D5 Render. You receive PDF plans, JPG renders, MP4 walkthroughs and takeoff spreadsheets, so you don't need any of that software.

    Will the plants suit my region?

    We build the plant palette from your climate zone and your client's brief. You confirm nursery availability in your area before ordering.

    Do you cover grading, drainage, irrigation or structural work?

    We show them conceptually, so homeowners and your crew can see the intent. We don't provide engineering, stamped drawings or permit sets. You verify site conditions, codes, HOA rules and utilities before building.

    You're in the Philippines. How do time zones and replies work?

    Our day overlaps Australian business hours. US contractors send notes at the end of their day and find progress when they're back on the job. Kim Daryl replies within hours.

    How do I pay?

    By card or bank transfer through a Wise or Payoneer invoice. Your first project is paid upfront. Memberships are billed monthly in advance.

    How do credits work?

    Each Evergreen membership gives you credits every month. A project uses credits by size: . Unused credits roll over one month, capped at one month's credits. Need more? Extra credits are charged at your plan's credit price.

    Can I cancel a membership?

    Yes, with 30 days' notice. Your founding rate stays locked for months while your membership is active.

    How big is your team?

    Small, on purpose. Edmond personally designs and reviews every project, and Kim Daryl handles your account and takeoffs. We'll add designers only when quality holds.
