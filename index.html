Questions

Is the work white-label?

Yes. We deliver under your logo, or co-branded if you prefer. We never contact your clients unless you ask us to.

How fast is turnaround?

Standard turnaround is [X days] from when we have complete site info. Rush delivery is [available / $___].

You're in the Philippines. How do time zones work?

Our workday overlaps with Australian business hours and US early mornings or evenings. US clients often send notes at the end of the day and get updates the next morning. Calls are booked at a time that suits you.

What file formats do you deliver?

Plans as PDF, renders as high-resolution JPG, walkthroughs as MP4, and takeoffs as a spreadsheet or PDF. Other formats on request.

What do you need from me to start?

Site photos, rough measurements or a survey, your client's wish list, and a budget range. More detail means fewer revisions.

What don't you do?

Our designs are conceptual landscape design. You, the contractor, verify site conditions, local codes, HOA rules and utility locations before building. We do not provide stamped engineering, permit drawings or installation.
